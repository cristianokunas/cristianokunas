## 👋 Hello, I'm Cristiano

🎓 I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the **Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS)**, passionate about High-Performance Computing (HPC), scientific software, and reproducible research.

🔭 I’m currently working on **data compression for seismic simulations in HPC environments**, focusing on porting and accelerating solutions on heterogeneous architectures, including **AMD and NVIDIA GPUs**.

🖥️ Volunteer HPC Administrator at UFRGS's [**Laboratório de Processamento Paralelo e Distribuído** ](https://gppd-hpc.inf.ufrgs.br) — managing and improving **Slurm-based infrastructure** for academic research since **Jan 2022**. I handle system maintenance, resource provisioning, user support, and hardware/software updates.
